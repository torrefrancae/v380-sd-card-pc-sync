Username and password

Set these in the V380 app on Android or iOS. Then type the same ones here.

  1. Open the camera in the V380 app.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right of the camera.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Password.
  5. Tap Change device password.
  6. Set a username and a password. Enter the password twice so you do not forget it.

This is the camera login. It is not the V380 account email or account password.
